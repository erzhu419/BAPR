# V20 full-state specialist policy-stability preregistration

## Question and frozen boundary

V19 showed that copying the robust critic, target critic, and entropy state in
addition to the actor improves mean specialist-bank switching return, but it
failed its frozen per-cell retention gate. Training traces localize the
remaining variance to policy erosion after a valid full-controller fork. A
fresh-critic warmup did not help.

V20 changes only policy update/selection after full-state initialization. The
actuator-polarity environment, robust-source budget, specialist interaction and
critic-update budget, v5 posterior, safe-utility calibration, switching router,
and all v18/v19 holdouts remain unchanged and unopened.

## Arms and data

Fresh training seeds are `83003,83021,83039`. Each robust source trains for
1,400 iterations and 5.6M transitions. Every specialist copies actor, critic,
target critic, and alpha, resets all optimizer states and replay, and receives
700 more iterations, 2.8M transitions, and 175,000 critic updates.

- `full_state_final`: actor and alpha update every critic step; publish the
  final policy.
- `full_state_best`: same training, but publish the policy with the highest
  fixed-mode training-validation return observed every 50 iterations.
- `period2_best`: actor and alpha update every second critic step and publish
  the best training-validation policy.

The training-validation stream is generated by the trainer's independent
`seed+1000` evaluation environment. It is distinct from stationary calibration
`185001,185017,185033`, stationary holdout `185101,185117,185133`, and
switching holdout `185201,185217,185233`. No holdout selects a checkpoint.

## Decision rule

Each candidate must pass the existing robust-relative bank gate on all three
seeds: at least three of four stationary modes improve by 5%, safe switching
improves by 10% on all three switching streams, and termination does not
increase.

Against paired `full_state_final`, a candidate must have positive mean safe
switching difference, win at least two of three policy seeds and six of nine
switching events, improve the worst-seed safe gain, retain at least 95% of the
control specialist return in every stationary seed-mode cell, and not reduce
the total number of passing modes. The passing arm with higher mean switching
return is selected; `full_state_best` is preferred when within 2% because it
does not change update frequency.

Failure closes validation selection and actor-period thinning as explanations.
Passing only authorizes a new five-seed equal-policy-budget confirmation; it
does not revise the v18 result.

## Artifact policy

Scheduler tasks synchronize compact source controller state, selected policy
parameters, JSON provenance, and evaluation outputs. Replay buffers, optimizer
state, critics, and full specialist checkpoints remain remote and are used only
for scheduler-managed resume.

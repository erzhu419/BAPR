# Independent Swimmer polarity screen (frozen before returns)

This is a prospective **benchmark-feasibility screen**, not a BAPR efficacy test. It uses Brax 0.14.2 `Swimmer-v2` with the generalized backend, rather than tuning another HalfCheetah mode set. The robot, reward, gravity, observation, and 1,000-step horizon remain unchanged. The two commanded actuators have four persistent gain vectors `(+, +)`, `(-, +)`, `(+, -)`, and `(-, -)`; independent Gaussian actuator noise has standard deviation 0.02 per step. A mode holds for 250 steps; switches preserve simulator state.

Policy seeds are `91003, 91021, 91039`. For each seed, train two SAC actors from scratch with the same 8.4M interactions and 525,000 updates: a four-mode robust actor and a fixed-mode-0 canonical actor. Do not select checkpoints. No HalfCheetah policy or inverse estimator transfers to Swimmer. Evaluation uses deterministic actions and stochastic actuator noise, with event seeds `199201, 199217, 199233`, five 1,000-step episodes per event, and cyclic rotations of the fixed schedules `(0,1,2,3)`, `(3,0,1,2)`, and `(1,3,2,0)`.

Evaluate robust SAC, the uncompensated canonical actor, a true-mode-compensated canonical actor, and native fixed-mode-0 execution of that same actor. The true-mode route is privileged and not deployable. The oracle and native reference must agree under paired reset/noise streams; all evaluated episodes must reach the first switch and full horizon. The environment passes the headroom screen only if the mean oracle-minus-robust switching return is at least 10% of the positive robust mean and at least 5 return points, and at least two of three policy seeds have both a 10% and a 5-point improvement. If this fails, retain the environment as a negative result; do not fit an estimator or retune these modes. If it passes, freeze a separate Swimmer inverse estimator and evaluate causal compensation with new policy and event seeds against equal-budget SAC, ESCP, and RE-SAC. The present three seeds are exploratory and will not be pooled with that later confirmation.
